Transparent pricing

Clear pricing for outbound and inbound demand generation

Outbound: USD 1,500 per month, or EUR 1,500 per month in Europe, for cold email and/or cold LinkedIn. Inbound: USD 99 per hour, or EUR 99 per hour in Europe, with a minimum of 10 hours per week. Three-month minimum for both services. Software, data, advertising spend and other third-party costs are separate unless the written scope includes them.

How inbound work is billed

Inbound services: USD 99 / EUR 99 (Europe · EUR). Three-month minimum for both services. Inbound also has a minimum of 10 hours per week.

The written scope should identify the channel, account access, deliverables, expected weekly allocation and approval responsibilities. Hours are service fees; media spend and third-party costs remain separate.

What is not included

Software, third-party data and advertising spend are not included. The exact stack depends on the engagement and should be agreed before purchase. Cold calling, CRM implementation, website redevelopment and full-cycle closing are not silently bundled into either service.

If the requested channel or scope falls outside the stated service, we will identify that before an engagement rather than adding surprise charges later.

Choose the service by the bottleneck

Choose managed outbound when the priority is starting qualified conversations with identifiable B2B buyers. Choose inbound work when the priority is capturing existing demand through Google Ads, improving organic search visibility or making authoritative answers easier for search engines and AI systems to retrieve.

Neither model removes market risk. Results still depend on the offer, addressable demand, buyer timing, proof, measurement quality and the team's ability to progress a credible opportunity.
