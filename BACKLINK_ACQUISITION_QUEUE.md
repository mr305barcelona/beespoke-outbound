## Priority 1: free business citations

These are the strongest low-effort citation opportunities still available. They may not all pass PageRank, but they establish consistent entity, address, service, and website information across major discovery platforms.

### Google Business Profile

- Official route: https://www.about.google/business/
- Cost: free.
- Effort: one setup plus Google verification.
- Use: Beespoke Outbound Lead Generation; Passeig de Gracia 54, 08007 Barcelona, Catalunya, Spain; +34 665 022 686; https://outbound-lead-generation.com/
- Primary category: Marketing agency. Secondary categories only when the interface offers an accurate match.
- Important: do not create a duplicate if Google already has a listing. Verification method is chosen by Google.
- Current state: stopped. Beespoke is online-only and customers cannot visit the Passeig de Gracia office. Do not resume or attempt to force eligibility.

### Bing Places for Business

- Official route: https://www.bingplaces.com/
- Cost: free.
- Effort: low after the Google profile is verified because Bing may allow importing listing data.
- Sequence: Google first, then import or claim in Bing.
- Current state: paused with the other location-listing platforms because Beespoke is online-only. Bing Webmaster Tools is the appropriate non-location product for indexing and search diagnostics.

### Apple Business

- Official route: https://business.apple.com/
- Cost: free.
- Effort: one account and organization/location verification.
- Value: controls the place card and business identity across Apple Maps, Siri, Mail, Wallet, and related Apple surfaces.
- Sequence: start after the Google listing is submitted so the canonical business facts are already stable.
- Current state: paused because Beespoke is online-only. The in-app browser also blocks the portal; do not attempt a workaround.

## Priority 2: editorial-source platforms

These do not create guaranteed links. They replace cold outreach with inbound requests from writers and publishers. Respond only where Noah has genuine experience and can provide a specific, first-hand answer.

### Help a B2B Writer

- Official route: https://helpab2bwriter.com/support
- Current state: active. Signup was submitted with source/promotional emails enabled and ad personalization left off; Noah confirmed the email.
- Best topics: outbound pricing, appointment setting, founder-led outbound, LinkedIn prospecting, sales development economics, cybersecurity go-to-market.
- Cadence after activation: answer a maximum of two tightly relevant requests per week.

### Featured

- Official route: https://featured.com/
- Model: answer publisher questions and submit expert commentary for editorial consideration.
- Best profile proof: the 2026 pricing benchmark, pricing calculator, cybersecurity framework, and founder biography.
- Cadence: one or two high-fit answers per week; never mass-submit generic answers.

### Qwoted

- Official route: https://app.qwoted.com/
- Model: public expert profile plus calls for experts from journalists and media creators.
- Best profile settings: keep the profile public only if Noah wants it indexed; add the benchmark as a media asset; choose narrow B2B sales and outbound topics.
- Caution: pitches use credits and the platform is strict about relevance and authenticity. Every answer must be reviewed in Noah's voice before submission.

### SourceBottle

- Official route: https://www.sourcebottle.com/
- Model: source alerts and journalist requests.
- Priority: lower than Help a B2B Writer, Featured, and Qwoted because request relevance is broader.
- Use only for precise sales, consulting, founder, Barcelona business, or cybersecurity-GTM requests.

### Clutch contributed content

- Official route: https://clutch.co/service-providers
- Dependency: wait until the Beespoke company profile is published.
- Angle: a buyer-side article on why a single “average outbound agency price” is misleading, using the source-linked benchmark without turning it into a promotional post.

## Linkable assets and exact pitch angles

### Pricing benchmark

Destination: https://outbound-lead-generation.com/research/2026-b2b-outbound-pricing-benchmark/

Pitch:

> Beespoke reviewed 40 public B2B outbound price points from 12 providers and kept USD, EUR, and GBP in their native currencies. Every row identifies the billing model, published output, commitment, and provider-controlled source. The dataset deliberately avoids a misleading industry average because software capacity, managed execution, booked meetings, and held meetings are not interchangeable.

Use for: pricing comparisons, founder budgeting, sales-development economics, outsourced-SDR guides, and B2B services reporting.

### Cost and break-even calculator

Destination: https://outbound-lead-generation.com/guides/outbound-lead-generation-cost/

Pitch:

> This buyer-side calculator works backward from meetings, opportunity rate, win rate, revenue, and gross margin. It helps a team test whether an agency or SDR budget is economically defensible instead of comparing monthly retainers in isolation.

Use for: founder finance resources, RevOps newsletters, startup budgeting guides, and sales-planning articles.

### Cybersecurity outbound framework

Destination: https://outbound-lead-generation.com/industries/cybersecurity-lead-generation/

Pitch:

> The framework explains why “CISO” is not a usable ICP on its own. It segments cybersecurity buyers by operating context, triggering situation, affected team, buying role, and proof threshold, then shows how those distinctions change outreach.

Use for: cybersecurity founder resources, security-sales newsletters, go-to-market communities, and B2B messaging articles.

## No-go list

- Paid followed-link packages.
- Bulk directory submissions.
- Link exchanges at scale.
- Private blog networks or sites that exist primarily to sell placements.
- Generic guest-post blasts.
- Profiles that require false categories, invented locations, or exaggerated company data.
- Any action on Noah's LinkedIn account or company page.
- Any use of the connected Niagara Gmail account.

## Measurement

Track monthly:

- referring domains and target pages in an independent backlink tool plus GSC when its Links report catches up;
- referral sessions and `calendly_click` events in GA4;
- positions 4–20 for pricing, cost, outsourced SDR, and cybersecurity queries;
- whether a link is editorial, directory, profile, or owned/UGC so the authority gain is not overstated.
