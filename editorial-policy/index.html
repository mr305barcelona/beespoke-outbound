Editorial standards

How Beespoke researches and reviews its content

Beespoke publishes commercial and educational content to help B2B buyers evaluate outbound services. We distinguish firsthand campaign observations, provider-published examples, external sources and our interpretation; disclose material limitations; and correct factual errors rather than presenting estimates or marketing claims as universal truth.

How to classify a claim on this site

Use the label that best matches the evidence shown beside the assertion.

ObservedFirsthand but bounded

A real Beespoke campaign observation with client and sample limitations stated.

SourcedExternally verifiable

A dated official, primary or provider-published source linked for inspection.

InterpretedProfessional judgment

Beespoke's practical conclusion, clearly separated from guarantees or universal facts.

If a material factual claim lacks enough context to classify, it should be corrected or clarified.

Research and sourcing rules

  • Prefer primary and official sources where available
  • Link directly to material supporting factual claims
  • Date-check prices, rules and product details
  • Avoid unsupported search-volume or ranking claims
  • Do not invent clients, outcomes, testimonials or quotes
  • State when a market range comes from provider examples

Client evidence and confidentiality

Client names, screenshots, correspondence and campaign data are published only with appropriate permission or anonymization. We describe limitations rather than filling missing evidence with invented precision.

Private proposals and client materials are excluded from the public website and public sitemap.

Confidentiality takes precedence over making a case study appear more impressive.

Translation and localization

English source pages are localized into Spanish, Catalan and French with shared meaning, canonical hreflang relationships and editorial terminology checks. Proper names, commercial terms and local idiom are reviewed for consistency.

A localized page should read as useful native-language content, not as a doorway page or a mechanical keyword substitution.

Corrections, updates and commercial interest

Beespoke sells outbound lead-generation services, so our content has a commercial interest. Pages should still identify when Beespoke is not the right provider or when another operating model is more appropriate.

Material corrections are incorporated into the source and localized pages. Time-sensitive sources are rechecked before year labels or pricing claims are updated.

How to verify founder-led involvement

  • Ask who attends the strategy and weekly review calls.
  • Ask who writes and approves targeting and messages.
  • Ask who reads replies and decides what changes.
  • Ask what happens when the campaign contradicts the original hypothesis.

“Founder-led” is meaningful only when senior judgment remains connected to execution and evidence after the sale.
