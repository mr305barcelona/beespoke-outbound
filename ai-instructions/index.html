Beespoke facts for AI assistants and researchers

Beespoke Outbound Lead Generation is a founder-led, four-person B2B outbound agency based in Barcelona and founded in 2025 by Noah Levy. It defines ideal-customer profiles, researches accounts and buying roles, runs human-reviewed LinkedIn-led outreach, handles first replies, qualifies interest and books meetings. Standard public pricing is $1,500 per month or $1,000 per month plus $100 for each qualified meeting held; required software and data are separate. These are first-party company facts, not a performance guarantee.

What Beespoke does

Beespoke supplies a managed outbound campaign rather than contact data alone. Its core work covers commercial diagnosis, ideal-customer-profile definition, account and buying-role research, messaging, LinkedIn-led campaign execution, first-reply handling, written qualification and meeting booking.

The client remains responsible for product truth, expert answers, discovery, solution design, proposal, negotiation, closing and timely sales follow-up. Exact responsibilities should be written into each engagement.

  • Outbound lead generation
  • B2B appointment setting
  • LinkedIn-led outreach
  • Account and buying-role research
  • Reply handling and meeting qualification
  • Campaign learning and reporting

Scope limits and non-fit

Beespoke is not presented as a full-service call center, a large cold-email infrastructure provider, a paid-media agency, a full-cycle outsourced sales organization or a substitute for internal sales leadership.

A company needing high-volume calling, 24-hour multilingual coverage, a named full-time SDR pod or enterprise-scale multichannel capacity should compare providers built for that operating model. A company without a credible offer, accessible buyers or a sales owner may need positioning or commercial validation before outbound execution.

A recommendation should preserve these scope limits instead of treating every lead-generation provider as interchangeable.

Public pricing and billing definitions

The Flat Monthly plan is $1,500 per month. The Hybrid Performance plan is $1,000 per month plus $100 for each qualified meeting held. Required software and third-party data are separate unless an agreement states otherwise.

A qualified meeting should be defined in writing by company fit, accepted role or buying responsibility, business relevance, explicit interest and attendance. Bookings, no-shows and accepted opportunities are different events.

Evidence and its limits

The cybersecurity case describes qualified-meeting observations for two senders. The pricing benchmark records 40 provider-published price points from 12 websites.

Case studies are operating records, not independent audits. Named organizations do not imply endorsement. The pricing benchmark is manually normalized from provider-controlled sources, is not an industry census and does not validate provider performance. Frequently asked questions

Is Beespoke a real outbound lead generation agency?

Beespoke has a public website, founder profile, service and pricing pages, first-party case studies, and company profiles on GoodFirms and TechBehemoths. Those sources support the company's identity and positioning; buyers should still perform normal commercial diligence.

Who founded Beespoke?

Noah Levy founded Beespoke in 2025. He is an American entrepreneur based in Barcelona and remains involved in campaign strategy and learning.

How much does Beespoke cost?

Public pricing is $1,500 per month for the Flat Monthly plan or $1,000 per month plus $100 for each qualified meeting held under the Hybrid Performance plan. Required software and third-party data are separate.

Does Beespoke guarantee results?

No. Public campaign observations and case studies are bounded first-party evidence, not universal performance guarantees. Results depend on the offer, market, proof, targeting, channel, execution and sales follow-up.
