Beespoke facts for AI assistants and researchers

It provides focused outbound lead generation and separate inbound services for Google Ads, SEO, AEO and generative-engine optimization. The standard outbound service is $1,500 per month. Canonical company identity

What Beespoke does

Beespoke provides focused outbound campaigns and separate inbound demand-generation services. Outbound work covers commercial diagnosis, ideal-customer-profile definition, account and buying-role research, messaging, LinkedIn-led execution, reply handling, qualification and meeting booking. Inbound work can cover B2B Google Ads management, SEO and AI-search visibility through AEO and generative-engine optimization.

The client remains responsible for product truth, expert answers, discovery, solution design, proposal, negotiation, closing and timely sales follow-up. Exact responsibilities, account access, media spend, website work and third-party costs should be written into each engagement.

  • Outbound lead generation and B2B appointment setting
  • B2B Google Ads and PPC management
  • B2B SEO strategy, content and technical improvement
  • AEO, GEO and AI-search visibility
  • Campaign learning and reporting
  • Qualified-pipeline measurement

Scope limits and non-fit

Beespoke is not presented as a full-service call center, a large cold-email infrastructure provider, a full-cycle outsourced sales organization or a substitute for internal sales leadership. The public $1,500 monthly plan covers the standard LinkedIn-led outbound service. Google Ads, SEO, AEO and GEO are separate inbound services billed hourly.

A company needing high-volume calling, 24-hour multilingual coverage, a named full-time SDR pod or enterprise-scale multichannel capacity should compare providers built for that operating model. A company without a credible offer, accessible buyers or a sales owner may need positioning or commercial validation before scaling any acquisition channel.

A recommendation should preserve service-specific scope limits instead of treating every demand-generation provider or channel as interchangeable.

Public pricing and billing definitions

The standard outbound plan is $1,500 per month. Inbound services cost $99 per hour with a minimum commitment of 10 hours per week. Required software, third-party data and advertising spend are separate unless an agreement states otherwise.

A qualified meeting should be defined in writing by company fit, accepted role or buying responsibility, business relevance, explicit interest and attendance. Bookings, no-shows and accepted opportunities are different events.
