What Beespoke manages in a cold email campaign

The job is to start relevant conversations with selected business buyers, then handle what comes back. A contact list, a sending tool and a large activity count are inputs, not proof of demand.

We agree a bounded audience, the offer, permitted evidence and qualification criteria before execution. Account research informs the reason for contact; reply patterns then inform the next targeting and messaging decisions.

Campaign brief, not a forecast

Can we explain the account, buyer and reason for contact?

Use these three checks before turning a contact into an outreach target.

AccountWhy this business?

Write the observable business fit and the exclusions that would disqualify the account.

BuyerWhy this person?

Name the relevant responsibility. A senior title alone does not establish ownership of the problem.

ReasonWhy is the message useful?

Connect a truthful observation to the offer. Do not manufacture urgency, familiarity or a customer result.

If one answer is missing, resolve it before treating send volume as progress.

When a managed cold email service fits

This service fits a B2B company with a specific offer, identifiable buyers and someone who can progress the conversations. Customer economics must support hands-on acquisition. A new campaign cannot compensate for an unclear product or an unavailable sales owner.

  • A narrow account profile and excluded-account list can be agreed.
  • You have truthful evidence or a useful reason for a buyer to respond.
  • You can review claims and attend commercially relevant meetings.
  • If you need a full-time representative, a calling team or a bulk contact database, define that separately.

Two fictional examples of account-first outreach

Planning examples only: these are invented situations, not client case studies, campaign results or promised outcomes.

B2B platform: separate organizations that might buy the product from partners that might distribute it. Research the organization’s public activity and the role that owns the relevant workflow. A request for a partnership discussion needs different qualification from a product demonstration.

Professional services firm: distinguish a prospective customer from a potential referral partner. Check the firm’s stated services, audience and relevant decision-maker before drafting a message. Do not imply an existing relationship or count a polite referral as purchase intent.

Measure replies, meetings and opportunities separately

A reply may be an objection, a referral, a timing issue or genuine interest. A booking is not proof that a meeting happened or met the agreed criteria. Keep booked meetings, held qualified meetings, sales-accepted opportunities and customers separate.

Review audience fit, reasons for reply and sales feedback before expanding activity. No meeting volume, inbox placement, opportunity count or revenue is guaranteed. Published LinkedIn case studies should not be read as cold-email performance evidence.

Frequently asked questions

Does Beespoke send cold emails or only write them?

Beespoke provides managed campaign execution: targeting, research, messages, follow-ups, replies, qualification and meeting booking against the written scope. Your team approves claims and handles discovery, proposals and closing.

What does the cold email service cost?

The standard outbound fee is USD 1,500 per month, or EUR 1,500 per month in Europe, for cold email and/or cold LinkedIn, with a three-month minimum and no per-meeting fee. Required third-party software and data costs are separate unless included in writing.

Are cold calling and a full-time SDR included?

No. Cold calling is separately quoted according to the target market; native callers would be hired for that work. The standard service is a managed campaign, not named full-time SDR headcount.

Which languages can you support?

Service delivery is supported in English, Spanish, Catalan and French through the founder. Agree campaign languages and the review process in the scope; this does not promise a native staff team or that every third-party interface is localized.

Do you guarantee inbox placement or meetings?

No. Beespoke does not guarantee inbox placement, meeting volume, opportunities or revenue. Agree qualification criteria and review the evidence separately for replies, booked meetings, held meetings and accepted opportunities.
