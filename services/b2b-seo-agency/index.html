What Beespoke manages in a B2B SEO program

The program begins with commercial reality: what buyers need to decide, which markets matter, why the offer is credible and which outcomes sales will accept. Keyword volume is useful, but it does not replace buyer intent, competitive evidence or the economics of the service being sold.

Scope can include technical and indexation review, search-demand research, competitor analysis, page and topic architecture, content briefs and production, on-page optimization, internal linking, structured data, conversion paths, reporting and editorial refreshes. Authority building must use legitimate, reviewable methods; rankings are never sold as a certainty.

  • Technical crawl and indexation diagnosis
  • Buyer-query and SERP research
  • Service, comparison and decision-page planning
  • Original content and source development
  • Internal-link and entity architecture
  • Search, engagement and lead-quality measurement
Original prioritization diagnostic

Score an SEO opportunity on three independent axes

Volume alone is not a publish decision. A page earns priority when demand, winnability and business value overlap.

DemandIs a real buyer decision visible?

Use query language, SERP composition and sales questions to confirm that the topic has a stable job.

WinnabilityCan the page add distinctive evidence?

Compare authority, format, freshness and information gain—not only a software difficulty score.

ValueWould the right visit matter?

Map the page to an offer, next step, qualification rule and measurable downstream outcome.

Publish when all three are defensible. Otherwise research, combine, postpone or reject the opportunity.

Choose pages by the decision they help a buyer make

A useful B2B search portfolio is not a pile of near-duplicate articles. It normally needs a clear service page, problem and use-case pages, comparison or alternative pages, pricing and cost guidance, implementation content, proof, and answers to recurring diligence questions.

Assign one primary intent to each URL. Combine close variants when the search results and buyer decision overlap; separate them when the audience, answer or destination job is genuinely different. This protects the site from internal competition and gives each page a clear reason to exist.

Build technical foundations that make content eligible

Search engines need crawlable links, accessible pages, clear canonicals, appropriate index controls, stable status codes, mobile usability and enough performance to retrieve and render the content. Structured data can clarify supported entities and page types, but it does not replace visible content or guarantee a search feature.

Technical work should be prioritized by business and indexation impact. A long issue list is not a strategy: record the affected template or URLs, evidence, recommended change, risk, owner and validation method.

Passing technical checks makes a page eligible to compete. It does not create demand, prove expertise or remove the need for authority.

Create information competitors cannot reproduce cheaply

Original information can be a dataset, tested framework, decision calculator, annotated process, expert observation, transparent price, comparison method or carefully bounded operating record. It should help a buyer do something that a generic summary cannot.

Every material claim needs the right evidence label. Distinguish first-party observation, external source, illustrative example and opinion. Protect confidential client material: no private proposal, account name, search term, screenshot or performance figure should become public without permission.

Use internal links as a buyer journey

Internal links should connect related decisions rather than repeat the same exact anchor everywhere. A service page can lead to method, cost, comparison and proof; a guide can return to the service only when it is a sensible next step.

Audit orphaned pages, broken references, shallow hubs, excessive repeated links and pages whose strongest related resource is missing. Navigation should remain useful on desktop and mobile, and anchor text should describe the destination in plain language.

  • One discoverable path from a relevant hub
  • Contextual links to the next buyer question
  • No orphaned commercial pages
  • No forced links to unrelated services
  • Consistent localized alternatives
  • Regular link validation after publishing

Measure visibility separately from business outcomes

Report impressions, rankings, clicks and indexed pages as search visibility. Report engaged visits and key actions as on-site behavior. Report qualified inquiries, accepted opportunities and revenue as commercial outcomes. Do not combine the three layers into one success number.

A new page can gain impressions before it earns top positions, and average site position can fall while total query coverage expands. Review page-query pairs, not only sitewide averages. Improve snippets when a relevant page ranks where it can be clicked; improve relevance, evidence, internal links and authority when the page is still far from page one.

Frequently asked questions

What does a B2B SEO agency do?

A B2B SEO agency connects technical eligibility, buyer and keyword research, page architecture, content, internal linking, authority development, conversion paths and measurement. The work should support the full B2B research journey rather than chase traffic without commercial relevance.

How is B2B SEO different from B2C SEO?

B2B searches often involve narrower audiences, multiple decision-makers, longer sales cycles, higher-consideration offers and lower conversion counts. That makes intent, evidence, buying-group questions and downstream qualification especially important.

How much does Beespoke charge for B2B SEO?

Beespoke bills inbound services at $99 per hour with a minimum commitment of 10 hours per week. Third-party software, data, design, development and paid promotion are separate unless included in the written scope.

How long does B2B SEO take?

There is no universal timeline. Existing authority, technical condition, competition, crawl frequency, content quality and demand all matter. Early indexing and impression changes can occur before stable rankings, qualified leads or revenue.

Does SEO also help visibility in AI answers?

Often, yes. Google states that its normal SEO foundations remain relevant to AI Overviews and AI Mode and that pages must be indexed and eligible for a snippet. Other AI products have their own retrieval systems and crawler controls, so measurement should remain platform-specific.
