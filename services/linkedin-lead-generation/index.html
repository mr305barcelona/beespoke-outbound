A relevant reason to contact each person

The research should explain why this person is worth contacting and why the message might matter to them. A company development, a question they shared publicly, a relevant event or a genuine connection to their location can all supply context. None of those facts proves a buying need by itself.

We use a repeatable message structure, then write the substantive reason for contact for the individual. Mentioning a name or copying a recent post is not enough. The connection between the observation, your offer and a useful conversation must be understandable.

Follow-up should respect the original message. We generally favor an opening and one brief follow-up rather than a succession of longer pitches. The agreed plan can move to another suitable channel, with prior replies and opt-outs respected.

When LinkedIn is a sensible starting point

LinkedIn is useful when you can identify the relevant companies and buying roles, and there is reason to believe those people use the platform. Recent relevant activity is one signal for choosing where to start; it is not proof that someone will reply or buy.

If a suitable buyer is less active on LinkedIn and has a relevant professional email address, the agreed campaign may start with email instead. The standard outbound plan can cover cold email and/or LinkedIn. Calling is separately quoted by target market; advertising spend and required third-party tools or data are separate unless the written scope includes them.

From a reply to a useful sales handoff

We handle replies against the agreed fit criteria, clarify interest and arrange a useful conversation through your calendar link. Product questions or commercial exceptions that require your judgment come back to your team. A vague positive reply does not automatically become a qualified meeting.

The handoff should retain the reason for contact, the actual conversation and unresolved questions. Your team leads discovery, proposals and closing, then confirms attendance, fit and any accepted opportunity. Booked, canceled, held and qualified meetings remain distinct. A refusal is respected; a later follow-up needs an appropriate reason and context.

  • How are credentials protected and who can access them?

Frequently asked questions

What does Beespoke’s LinkedIn outreach service cost?

The standard outbound plan is USD 1,500 per month, or EUR 1,500 per month in Europe, with a three-month minimum and no per-meeting fee. It can cover cold email and/or LinkedIn as agreed. Required software, data and other third-party costs are separate unless included in the written scope. Cold calling is quoted separately by target market.

Do you sell a contact list or manage the campaign?

Beespoke manages the agreed account research, individual messages, follow-up, replies, qualification and booking. Your team approves the proposed targets and permitted claims, provides product judgment when needed, and leads discovery, proposals and closing.

Does every campaign have to start on LinkedIn?

No. Recent relevant LinkedIn activity can support starting there. When email is more suitable, the agreed outbound campaign can start by email instead. Channel selection follows the buyer and the reason for contact, rather than a rule to contact everyone everywhere.

How do you decide whether a meeting is qualified?

We agree company, role, responsibility and interest criteria before outreach. A booked time, an attended call, a qualified meeting and an accepted opportunity are recorded separately. Your team supplies the outcome of the sales conversation; a connection acceptance or polite reply is not sufficient.

Do you guarantee meetings, sales or zero account risk?

No fixed meeting or sales volume and no zero-risk account promise are included. Review the proposed access methods, tools, human checks and incident responsibilities against LinkedIn’s current rules before authorizing activity.

What should I prepare before booking a fit call?

An outline of your offer, intended companies and buying roles is enough to start the discussion. Bring public proof if available. You do not need to upload a private client list, campaign export or prospect contact details to book.
