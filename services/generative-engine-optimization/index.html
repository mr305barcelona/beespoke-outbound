GEO, AEO and AI-search visibility

Generative engine optimization and AEO for credible B2B discovery

Generative engine optimization (GEO), answer engine optimization (AEO), AI SEO and LLM SEO are overlapping labels for work that improves a brand's eligibility, clarity and usefulness when search and AI systems retrieve sources. Beespoke combines technical search foundations, entity consistency, direct answers, original evidence, legitimate third-party citations and referral attribution. No agency can guarantee that a model will mention, cite or recommend a company.

Define the work before choosing the acronym

AEO traditionally focuses on concise answers and search answer features. GEO commonly refers to inclusion or citation in generative answers. AI SEO and LLM SEO are broader market terms. The boundaries are not standardized, so an engagement should specify platforms, queries, pages, technical work, evidence, off-site authority and measurement instead of relying on the label.

For Google AI features, Google says the same foundational SEO practices remain relevant and there is no special markup or additional technical requirement. A supporting page must be indexed and eligible to appear in Search with a snippet. Other systems have their own crawlers, indexes and controls.

Audit the three layers an AI answer needs

Treat visibility as a chain. A failure at any layer can prevent a useful mention even when the page looks polished.

EligibilityCan the source be crawled and retrieved?

Check indexation, crawler access, snippet controls, canonicalization and stable page delivery for each target system.

VerifiabilityCan the entity and claim be corroborated?

Align visible facts, structured relationships, author identity, external profiles and primary sources.

UsefulnessDoes the passage improve the answer?

Provide direct language, original evidence, limitations, dates and a clear reason to cite this source.

Measure each layer independently. A mention is not automatically a citation, a citation is not automatically a visit, and a visit is not automatically a qualified lead.

Establish a platform and query baseline

Begin with the questions a real buyer might ask when discovering, comparing and validating providers. Test a repeatable prompt set across agreed systems and record whether the brand appears, which competitors appear, which sources are cited, whether facts are correct and how volatile the answers are.

Prompt testing is directional, not a universal ranking report. Answers can vary by time, model, location, personalization and retrieval state. Preserve the exact question, date, platform and result so later changes can be compared honestly.

Make the entity easy to verify

A model or search system should encounter the same operating name, website, founder relationship, service definitions, location, contact details and evidence boundaries across the site and legitimate external profiles. Contradictory names and inflated categories make verification harder.

Use visible facts first, then accurate structured data where it represents the page. Organization, Person, Service, Article and Breadcrumb markup can clarify relationships for systems that support them, but schema is not a shortcut to citations and should never assert reviews, awards, locations or services that are not real.

Create evidence worth retrieving and citing

Direct answers help a system locate a useful passage, but citation-worthiness comes from information that is specific, attributable and hard to replace: original data, transparent methodology, worked decision tools, expert explanations, current source synthesis and bounded operating evidence.

Separate observation from inference. Link primary sources close to factual claims, state when they were checked and disclose commercial interest. Do not publish private client material or disguise an illustrative scenario as a case study.

Answer formatting can improve extractability. It cannot turn an unsupported claim into authoritative evidence.

Earn corroboration outside the website

Legitimate company profiles, association listings, expert contributions, partner pages, interviews and independent references can help people and machines verify that an entity exists and is connected to a topic. Quality and factual consistency matter more than bulk directory count.

Avoid fabricated profiles, fake reviews, self-created third-party praise, automated guest-post networks and paid links that exist only to manipulate rankings. The safest authority work creates a genuine public record that would remain useful even if search engines ignored the link.

  • Consistent independent company profiles
  • Expert answers with a real author identity
  • Original datasets others can reference
  • Partner or customer mentions with permission
  • Current source citations and corrections
  • No invented reviews, awards or clients

Measure visibility without inventing an AI ranking

Track platform-specific mentions and citations for the same prompt set, cited URLs, AI referral sessions, engaged visits, key actions, qualified leads and assisted conversions. Search Console can report Google Search performance, while Bing Webmaster Tools has introduced AI citation reporting for supported Microsoft experiences.

Referral data is incomplete: some AI-assisted discovery may arrive as direct traffic or occur before a later branded search. Ask new leads how they found the company and preserve their answer alongside analytics instead of forcing false precision. Report citations, visits and commercial outcomes separately.

Frequently asked questions

What is generative engine optimization?

Generative engine optimization is the practice of improving a source's technical eligibility, clarity, evidence and authority so generative systems can retrieve and cite it when relevant. The term is evolving and does not describe one standardized ranking system.

What is answer engine optimization?

Answer engine optimization focuses on making accurate answers easy to identify, understand and present in search or answer experiences. It overlaps with SEO and GEO through clear structure, direct responses, supporting evidence and technical accessibility.

Is GEO different from SEO?

The emphasis differs, but the foundations overlap. SEO commonly targets visibility and visits from search results; GEO emphasizes inclusion or citation in generative answers. Google states that its established SEO guidance remains relevant to its AI features.

Can an agency guarantee citations in ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews or Grok?

No. Platforms control retrieval and answer generation, outputs can vary, and eligibility is not a promise of inclusion. An agency can improve crawl access, entity clarity, content usefulness, corroboration and measurement, but should not guarantee mentions or recommendations.
