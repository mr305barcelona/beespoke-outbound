Dedicated SDR, SDR pod or managed campaign?

Outsourced SDR is used for different services. A dedicated representative supplies named capacity; an SDR pod may combine researchers, callers, writers and managers; a managed campaign owns a defined channel and workflow. Ask for the actual allocation and responsibility map.

What is needed before the campaign starts

Bring a clear offer, the target company and buyer profile, market and language requirements, available proof, excluded accounts and a named sales owner. Agree which account or profile access is needed, who approves messages and how product questions are answered.

Research and preparation come before outreach. Timing depends on access, approvals and whether the targeting and message can be supported with evidence; there is no promised appointment date. Review the first conversations for buyer fit, objections and message relevance before broadening the segment.

  • Approve target criteria and excluded accounts.
  • Approve truthful claims and the message route.
  • Write the meeting definition and handoff fields.
  • Set review dates, escalation ownership and a stopping rule.

A booking needs qualification and sales context

Write the company profile, buyer responsibility, business relevance, explicit interest and attendance rules. Keep positive replies, booked meetings, qualified meetings actually held and opportunities accepted by sales as separate stages. Agree how duplicates, cancellations, no-shows and excluded accounts are handled.

A useful handoff records the account and contact, why they fit, the conversation history, criteria checked, stated interest, unanswered questions and meeting status. Your sales owner then accepts or rejects an opportunity with a reason. A booking alone is not evidence of pipeline or a sale.

How to verify founder-led involvement

  • Ask who attends the strategy and weekly review calls.
  • Ask who writes and approves targeting and messages.
  • Ask who reads replies and decides what changes.
  • Ask what happens when the campaign contradicts the original hypothesis.

“Founder-led” is meaningful only when senior judgment remains connected to execution and evidence after the sale.

SDR as a service, fractional SDR and managed campaigns

SDR as a service and sales development outsourcing describe ways to transfer prospecting work. They do not specify who researches, writes, handles replies or owns the sales handoff. Ask for those tasks and the actual availability in writing. A fractional SDR usually means a share of a person’s capacity; that is a different purchase from a managed campaign with agreed responsibilities.

Beespoke manages cold email and/or LinkedIn prospecting. We research the people, propose the targets for client approval, write individual messages and configure agreed outreach when authorized. Reply handling, qualification and calendar coordination are part of the defined workflow. This does not automatically supply a dedicated part-time employee, full-time SDR, calling team or outsourced closer. Compare the work and retained decisions before comparing labels.
