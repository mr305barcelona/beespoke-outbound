Why broad consulting positioning fails

Buyers rarely search for generic expertise. They respond to a recognizable problem, consequence and reason to trust the firm in their context.

A consultancy offering strategy, transformation, technology and growth to every industry does not give outbound enough specificity to create relevance.

Narrowing the campaign is not the same as narrowing the entire firm. It creates one testable route into the market.

Choose a market wedge

  • One costly or urgent business problem
  • A definable company situation or trigger
  • A reachable buying committee
  • A credible diagnostic or point of view
  • Evidence appropriate to the claim
  • A project value that supports human acquisition

Map consulting buying committees

The economic buyer, problem owner, internal champion, evaluator and procurement stakeholder may be different people. Seniority alone does not reveal who feels the problem or can sponsor change.

Illustrative consulting buying roles
RoleWhat they evaluateUseful campaign evidence

ChampionInternal credibility and feasibilityPractical next step and stakeholder map
ProcurementScope and supplier riskClear deliverables and commercial structure

Combine authority with direct outreach

Consulting sales depends on trust. Useful research, diagnostics, cases, events and referrals can make direct outreach more credible; outbound should distribute a point of view rather than replace one.

LinkedIn is especially useful when senior buyers and visible professional context matter, but it should not be treated as the only demand channel.
