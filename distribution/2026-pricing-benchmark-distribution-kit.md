## Approved one-line methodology

Beespoke reviewed 40 publicly listed B2B outbound price points from 12 providers, preserved each provider's native currency, recorded what the price actually included, and linked every row to its public source.

## Citeable facts

These statements can be reused verbatim or paraphrased:

1. The dataset contains 40 public price points from 12 providers.
2. The offers use three native currencies: USD, EUR, and GBP.
3. Beespoke made zero currency conversions, avoiding exchange-rate assumptions and false precision.
4. The lowest price in the dataset is $150 per month for one AI-assisted email account. It describes software-like capacity, not a managed SDR service.
5. The highest published managed LinkedIn tier in the dataset is $12,000–$15,000 per month for six to eight dedicated profiles with senior support.
6. The observed billing models include fixed monthly fees, hybrid base-plus-outcome fees, and one-time charges.
7. Published outputs are not interchangeable: they include account or sending capacity, leads, booked meetings, held meetings, and team-delivered campaign work.
8. Commitment terms and included work vary enough that headline price alone is not a reliable comparison.
9. Every benchmark row includes a source so readers can inspect the provider's own public offer.
10. Sources were checked on July 24, 2026.

Do not turn these observations into an “industry average.” The sample compares public offers with materially different scopes and output definitions.

### LinkedIn draft 1 — the comparison problem

Outbound pricing is difficult to compare because the headline numbers often describe different products.

A low monthly price may cover software-like sending capacity. Another offer may include research, copy, reply handling, and a delivery team. A meeting fee may apply to a booking, a held meeting, or a qualified attended conversation.

We reviewed 40 public price points from 12 B2B outbound providers and recorded:

- native currency
- billing model
- published output
- commitment notes
- row-level source

We deliberately did not calculate a single “industry average.” The offers are too different for that to be honest.

The full benchmark and methodology are here:
https://outbound-lead-generation.com/research/2026-b2b-outbound-pricing-benchmark/

### LinkedIn draft 2 — one useful number

The lowest public price in our outbound benchmark is $150/month.

The highest published managed LinkedIn tier is $12,000–$15,000/month.

That spread does not show a chaotic market. It shows that “outbound service” can mean radically different things—from one AI-assisted email account to six or eight dedicated LinkedIn profiles with senior support.

Before comparing price, normalize:

1. What work is included?
2. What does the published output mean?
3. Is the result booked, held, or qualified?
4. What commitment is required?
5. Which software and data costs sit outside the fee?

Our 40-row, source-linked comparison:
https://outbound-lead-generation.com/research/2026-b2b-outbound-pricing-benchmark/

### LinkedIn draft 3 — methodology

We published a B2B outbound pricing benchmark, but avoided the most tempting statistic: a market average.

The 40 public offers in the dataset use three currencies and several billing models. More importantly, their outputs are not equivalent. Some sell capacity. Some sell campaign execution. Some price booked or held meetings. Some include multiple dedicated profiles.

Converting all of that into one number would create precision without comparability.

Instead, every row keeps its native currency and records the scope, output definition, commitment, and source.
