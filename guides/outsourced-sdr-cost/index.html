2026 outsourced SDR cost guide

How much does an outsourced SDR cost?

Published 2026 outsourced SDR prices commonly range from roughly $1,500 to $12,000 per month depending on location, seniority, channel scope and whether the offer is a supplied rep, managed campaign or full pod. Compare fully loaded scope, ramp and accepted opportunities—not monthly fees alone.

Build the fully loaded monthly SDR number

Add every layer; do not compare a salary with an all-inclusive provider fee.

Provider layerRetainer + variable fees

Setup allocation, base fee, per-meeting fees, minimums and currency effects.

Operating layerTools + data + infrastructure

CRM, contact data, dialer, sending domains, LinkedIn and reporting systems.

Client layerManagement + enablement

Strategy, training, reviews, sales follow-up and time spent correcting work.

Fully loaded cost per accepted opportunity = all three layers ÷ opportunities accepted by sales in the same period.

A quote tells you the expected monthly charge. Ask for two sample invoices for the same scope. Use one for a normal month and one for a month when the assigned SDR is unavailable. This shows whether the fee buys reserved capacity, work delivered or a defined result. It also gives you a place to check charges for tools, onboarding and work your team still does.

Work through a fictional replacement month

Assume the proposal reserves 100 hours of SDR work for the month. The assigned rep misses 20 hours, and the backup delivers 10. That leaves 90 hours delivered and 10 uncovered. Confirm who handled replies during the gap and whether the backup's work met the same acceptance criteria. The staffing record shows what happened. The contract tells you whether the 10 uncovered hours earn a credit, can be made up later or stay payable.

Calculate the total for that month using the existing cost stack. Add separate tool charges and client time once. Apply a credit only when the contract supports it and the provider shows it on the invoice. A later make-up session counts when delivered. Keep it attached to the original gap so it is visible without being counted twice.

Keep the invoice and sales measures separate

Ask both providers to reconcile the invoice with the same reporting period and acceptance rules. If six qualified meetings were held and sales accepted four opportunities, calculate the two unit costs separately. A pending opportunity stays open until sales can review it. If nothing was accepted, report the spend and zero accepted opportunities. There is no cost per accepted opportunity to calculate for that period.

Before signing, have each bidder fill in the same sample. Record the person who approves extra costs, who covers replies when the SDR is absent and when the buyer can request a review. This example uses fictional hours and outcomes. Use your own quotes, records and costs for a decision.

Compare the two invoices

Assume USD 5,000 for reserved time, USD 200 for separately billed tools and USD 300 of buyer time in both months. Normal month: USD 5,200 on the invoice, or USD 5,500 including buyer time. Replacement month: if the contract credits USD 50 per uncovered hour, the 10 uncovered hours yield a USD 500 credit; the invoice is USD 4,700 and the total cost is USD 5,000. Without that clause the credit is not automatic. If the hours are made up instead, record them when delivered and do not also subtract the example credit.

Do you automatically get a credit if the rep is replaced?

No. Check whether the agreement covers reserved hours or delivered work, who approves a credit or a make-up session, and where that adjustment appears on the invoice.

Buyer check. Ask who covers live replies, which costs are already included, and what the contract says about credit or make-up work.

When a cheaper SDR costs more

Low-cost capacity is uneconomic if the client must rewrite every message, rebuild every list or supervise basic execution. Equally, premium teams are wasteful when the offer cannot support their scope.

The right unit is cost for usable sales-development capability, not cost for a person-shaped line item.

Ask what would remain if the engagement ended tomorrow: data, systems, learning, copy and pipeline.

How to verify founder-led involvement

  • Ask who attends the strategy and weekly review calls.
  • Ask who writes and approves targeting and messages.
  • Ask who reads replies and decides what changes.
  • Ask what happens when the campaign contradicts the original hypothesis.

“Founder-led” is meaningful only when senior judgment remains connected to execution and evidence after the sale.
