Choosing outsourced SDR support

Best outsourced SDR companies: shortlist by operating model, not hype

There is no universal best outsourced SDR company. The right provider depends on whether you need enterprise SDR capacity, a focused founder-led campaign, phone-heavy appointment setting, LinkedIn specialization or a multichannel team. This guide groups established providers by their publicly described model, names first-party sources and gives buyers a repeatable way to validate scope, people, qualification, ownership and cost. Inclusion is not an endorsement, ranking or claim of performance.

Three checks before choosing finalists

Record evidence before choosing finalists. Mark an essential requirement as demonstrated, awaiting clarification or confirmed mismatch. A total score cannot settle a missing essential term.

Operating-model fit

The channels, capacity and client responsibilities match the work.

Inspectable execution

Research, messages, reply decisions and outcomes can be reviewed.

A workable exit

Ownership, exports, notice and access removal are clear.

Compare complete costs over the same period once the essential scope is clear. An unanswered question needs clarification; it is not proof of poor service.

A completed buying exercise: three proposals, one provisional choice

Entirely fictional buying exercise. No supplier below represents a named provider, customer or prospect. The records and outcomes are invented to explain a decision, not to forecast results.

Start with the work your team cannot cover

A small B2B software team sells to operations leaders. It has a CRM and a sales owner who takes discovery calls, but nobody owns account research or reply triage. The agreed initial channels are email and LinkedIn. The team can approve targeting and answer product questions; it cannot coach a dedicated SDR every day.

Scope to transfer
Account research, individually relevant messages, approved sending and reply triage.
Client contribution
Approve roles, regions and exclusions; resolve product questions; attend sales calls and close.
Qualified meeting
The attendee has the agreed buying responsibility, matches the approved account criteria, expresses relevant interest and actually attends. Record an accepted opportunity separately.
Evidence before signature
A delivery-owner demonstration, backup coverage, a sample CRM record and an export with access-removal terms.

Read the proposals, then make a decision

Managed campaign

Evidence in this fictional proposal
The proposed delivery lead demonstrates research and reply triage in a fictional CRM record and names a backup role. The proposal assigns list approval and sales calls to the buyer. Export ownership is mentioned but no sample or exit clause is supplied.
Decision for this brief
Keep as a conditional finalist. The work matches; the exit requirement is still unverified. Do not sign on the strength of the demonstration alone.
What would change it
Ask the delivery owner for a usable sample export and written ownership, access-removal and notice terms. Only treat that requirement as satisfied after inspection.

Dedicated SDR seat

Evidence in this fictional proposal
The proposal supplies a rep, while the client is expected to coach daily and own list research and reply escalation. The buyer has no manager available for that work.
Decision for this brief
Exclude under the current brief: a confirmed responsibility mismatch. This does not establish that the supplier is poor at its stated service.
What would change it
Reconsider if the buyer appoints a manager and funds the retained work, or the supplier revises the actual scope in writing.

Calling support

Evidence in this fictional proposal
The proposal covers calls and call dispositions. It does not cover the agreed email/LinkedIn work, research or inbox replies.
Decision for this brief
Exclude under the current brief: the purchased work would not fill the gap. Do not score phone volume as evidence for the missing channels.
What would change it
Rebrief only if calling becomes an agreed need. Confirm language, market, native-caller requirements and separate cost before comparing that scope.

The filled decision record

Provisional choice
The managed campaign remains a finalist, not an awarded contract.
Unresolved requirement
Export and exit evidence: no sample or clause has been inspected.
Next owner
The buyer’s sales owner requests the missing material from the delivery lead.
Stop or reopen
Do not sign if the essential ownership term remains unresolved. Reopen the model decision if client management capacity or agreed channels change.

Rehearse an uncertain reply before counting a meeting

“Someone else handles this. Can you tell me what it is about?”

Record this as an open referral, not a qualified meeting. Keep the original reply and route, verify the referred person’s role, decide whether a relevant answer is justified, and name who handles it. A job title, forwarded message or calendar invitation alone does not establish qualification. Request a redacted work sample with permission; never collect passwords, private lead lists or unnecessary contact details for a buying exercise.

Agree the review and exit before signature

Inspect an export containing account identifiers, message versions, reply history and dispositions. Agree a review date, continuation evidence, cancellation notice and who can revoke access. Return to the decision if backup coverage or promised ownership cannot be demonstrated.

What the evidence actually supports

4 original bookings; 2 held meetings; 1 qualified, held meeting; 1 rescheduled meeting still pending; 1 cancelled booking. Opportunity and revenue outcomes remain unknown. These categories overlap: the one qualified meeting is part of the two held meetings, not an extra meeting.

Do not turn this tiny, unfinished example into a conversion benchmark or a supplier forecast. Compare cohorts with the same original booking dates, observation cutoff and qualification rules. Ask the provider to reproduce its claim from permitted, redacted records and document disagreements. A dashboard total alone does not resolve them.

Verify the people who will perform the work

A provider brand does not tell you who researches accounts, writes messages, sends outreach, handles replies or joins reviews. Ask for roles, locations, seniority, capacity, replacement process and the amount of management spread across clients.

Inspect a real work sample with sensitive information removed. The sample should show the account rationale, evidence used, role selection, message route and qualification decision—not only a polished dashboard.

  • Named account lead and escalation owner
  • Researcher, writer, sender and reply owner
  • Approximate client load by role
  • Training and coaching cadence
  • Replacement and continuity procedure
  • Client approval points

Normalize scope and pricing

One outsourced SDR offer may supply named full-time capacity. Another may sell a shared campaign team, meetings, contact volume or software-assisted execution. Normalize the model before comparing fees.

Record setup, minimum term, tools, data, domains or profiles, channel capacity, fixed and variable charges, client time, billing event and cancellation terms. Use held, in-ICP meetings and accepted opportunities as separate outcome fields.

Test qualification and sales handoff

Ask the provider to qualify one hypothetical meeting using the proposed contract language. The exercise exposes whether a job title is being mistaken for buying responsibility and whether interest, timing or business relevance must be present.

The handoff should include the original context, message history, attendee role, stated problem, qualification notes, scheduling status and any commitments made. The client's sales owner should be able to accept or reject the opportunity using documented reasons.

A booked calendar event is not automatically a qualified meeting, and a qualified meeting is not automatically pipeline.

Protect data, accounts and learning

Confirm which CRM, inboxes, profiles, phone numbers and data sources are used and who owns them. Set access controls, approved claims, opt-out handling, platform escalation and data-return requirements.

A strong provider leaves the buyer with a reviewable campaign record: account logic, evidence, messages, reply categories, outcomes and recommendations. Without that record, the buyer rents activity but does not build an acquisition asset.

  • Client-accessible CRM or export
  • Account and contact provenance
  • Approved message and claims library
  • Access removal and credential handover
  • Reply and disqualification taxonomy
  • End-of-engagement learning report

Provider references and disclosure

Verify each provider’s current scope on its own website. Public offers change. Request relevant references and review the contract and data-handling terms before signing.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best outsourced SDR company?

No provider is best for every buyer. Match the provider's operating model, channels, geography, team structure, qualification standard and commercial terms to the job you need done, then verify delivery evidence.

How were companies selected for this list?

They publicly market outsourced SDR, appointment-setting or closely related B2B lead-generation services and appeared in the market sources reviewed. Inclusion is unpaid, not a performance certification and not a fixed rank.

How much do outsourced SDR companies charge?

Pricing varies by dedicated versus shared staffing, geography, channels, data, management and output definition. Use a fully loaded monthly cost and a consistent held-qualified-meeting denominator.

Is Beespoke ranked first because it published this page?

No. Beespoke is disclosed as a commercial participant and appears as one model in an unranked shortlist. Buyers should choose another provider when its scale, channels or operating model fit better.
