Separate audit, implementation and ongoing work

An audit identifies access, content and measurement gaps; it does not automatically fix them. Implementation resolves agreed technical issues and creates or improves pages. An ongoing program updates evidence, expands useful coverage and follows changes in visibility and leads. Ask which stage the quote covers, who edits the website and how many review cycles are included. A monitoring subscription records observations; it is not a substitute for implementing improvements.

AEO procurement worksheet

Ask for work, evidence and a boundary

Use these three questions with every finalist.

1What changes?

List the exact pages, technical corrections and source updates, with owners and approval steps.

2What can I inspect?

Require before-and-after URLs, prompt records, cited sources and qualified-lead reporting, not only a proprietary score.

3What remains outside the fee?

Name software, development, design, interviews, paid promotion and client time. State who owns the assets at exit.

Guaranteed AI citations are not a controllable deliverable. Agree the work and measurement instead.

What should increase the price?

More markets, languages, complex products and technical restrictions create real work. Original research, interviews, content review and development also require time. Ask the agency to connect each extra fee to a deliverable or dependency. Publishing many near-identical answer pages adds volume without necessarily improving usefulness. Price legitimate authority work by the activity and evidence produced, rather than by guaranteed placement in an AI answer.

Measure four outcomes separately

A brand mention is not a citation; a citation is not a website visit; a visit is not a qualified lead. Record the platform, prompt, date, answer and cited URL for visibility checks. Use comparable prompts across languages and label the sampling limitations. In analytics, separate known AI referrals from direct and search traffic. Reconcile qualified bookings with the preserved source and the visitor’s optional answer. Do not equate a monitor’s score with a universal AI ranking.

Example: plan an initial four-week allocation

Illustration only: a 40-hour allocation might reserve eight hours for diagnosis and measurement, eight for technical and entity corrections, sixteen for priority content, and eight for review and reporting. At $99 per hour this is $3,960, excluding tools and third parties. This is a planning example, not a promised package or delivery timetable. Existing technical debt, research availability and approval delays can change the allocation. Agree what is deferred if a dependency consumes more time.

Avoid paying for unsupported promises

Google states that its existing SEO practices apply to AI features and that no special structured data guarantees inclusion. Require the provider to identify the documented mechanism behind each proposed technical change. Correct inaccurate business facts and weak answers because they affect readers and retrieval, not because an agency claims a secret AI signal. A credible scope specifies controllable work, reviewable outputs and the platform-dependent limits of results.

Frequently asked questions

Can an AEO agency guarantee ChatGPT recommendations?

No. Platforms control retrieval and answer generation. An agency can improve access, factual clarity, evidence and measurement, but cannot promise a specific recommendation or citation.
