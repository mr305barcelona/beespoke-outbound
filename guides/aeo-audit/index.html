Evidence-led AI search review

AEO audit checklist for B2B: measure AI search visibility with evidence

An AEO audit examines how answer engines describe your business, which pages they cite and whether relevant visits become genuine buyer conversations. Start with real buying questions and a repeatable observation log. Separate brand mentions, exact URL citations, referral sessions and bookings. Record the model, date and conditions; missing evidence is unassessable, not zero. An audit can guide improvements, but cannot guarantee inclusion in an AI answer.

Define the buyer questions and the audit boundary

Choose questions a potential buyer would ask before selecting a service: what the service includes, who it fits, how to compare approaches and what evidence to request. Include questions without your brand as well as questions about it. Keep branded accuracy checks separate from unbranded discovery.

Write a fixed prompt set for each market and language. Record the exact wording and why the question matters. Avoid a large collection of near-identical prompts that makes a percentage look precise without covering different decisions. Do not put private customer, prospect or account information into a public model.

Make each observation reproducible

For every run, record engine and visible model/version, timestamp, language, location if known, signed-in or clean context, exact prompt and whether browsing or sources were available. Preserve a concise answer excerpt or reference where appropriate. If a setting or model version is not disclosed, write unknown.

Personalization, model updates and answer variability limit comparisons. Repeat the same questions under matched conditions across documented review dates; do not compare one personalized answer with an unrelated clean session as though only your page changed. Manual observation is a sample, not a complete census of AI search.

  • Capture whether the brand is mentioned and what is said about it.
  • Record each cited URL exactly and verify whether it supports the associated claim.
  • Mark source-panel availability separately from citation presence.
  • Log factual errors, unsupported statements and inaccessible evidence without filling gaps by inference.

Keep mentions, citations, referrals and bookings separate

A mention means the answer names the business. A citation means a source link identifies a particular URL. A referral means an observed visit from an AI source. A genuine booking is a separate event that needs scheduling evidence. None of these proves the next stage occurred.

Check factual accuracy and ordinary search eligibility

Review your own service explanations, organization facts, authorship, source dates and language consistency. The page should answer the buyer’s question plainly, show useful original help and distinguish approved facts from examples. Accurate visible content and matching structured data make the information more coherent; they do not force an engine to cite it.

Google’s AI-features guidance says the normal SEO foundations apply. Supporting pages need to be indexed and eligible to appear with a snippet. There is no special AI file or schema required for these features. Check access, indexability, canonical references and internal discovery before adding speculative technical files.

A page being crawlable is not proof of indexing; indexing is not proof of citation. Do not block useful search access as an untested visibility tactic or treat an llms.txt file as a ranking switch.

Review answer gaps and competing sources carefully

Inspect the pages actually cited for your buyer question when source links are available. Note which decision they help with, their evidence and what your existing page misses. A result that names a competitor does not establish why that competitor appeared. An unavailable source panel does not prove there were no citations.

Improve the relevant existing page when it can answer the question well: clarify scope, direct answers, responsibilities, sourced distinctions and useful examples. Create a new URL only for a distinct buyer job. Do not copy a cited page, invent client proof or add broad claims merely to resemble a sampled answer.

Connect AI visibility to the acquisition record

Use source-aware analytics and original scheduling records to distinguish new website-acquired calls from outreach-led calls that later visit the site. Retain known landing pages and booking identifiers only with the necessary access and purpose. A Direct visit with no source cannot safely be assigned to AI, and one ChatGPT booking does not establish a conversion benchmark.

Exclude identified internal or audit visits and retain attribution gaps explicitly. Compare matched periods and mature booking cohorts, including cancellations, calls held and qualification. Report observation coverage alongside any share of sampled answers; never label the sample as market-wide AI visibility.

Finish with a bounded action plan

Rank proposed actions by verified factual risk, relevant buyer value, evidence strength, effort and dependencies. Name the page, exact change, responsible owner and next observation date. Correct a demonstrated false claim promptly; test an uncertain visibility hypothesis with a baseline rather than presenting it as a proven cause.

Frequently asked questions

What should an AEO audit measure?

Buyer-question coverage, sampled brand mentions, exact cited URLs, factual accuracy and source-panel availability, alongside ordinary search eligibility, observed AI referrals and genuine new bookings. Keep each measure and its coverage separate.

What is the difference between an AEO audit and an AI visibility audit?

The names overlap in practice. Define the actual scope: engines, prompts, languages, source inspection, technical review and outcome reconciliation. The label alone does not tell you what evidence is collected.

Does Google require special AI schema or llms.txt?

Google’s cited AI-features guidance states there is no special schema or AI file requirement. Normal SEO foundations and indexed, snippet-eligible supporting pages remain relevant. That does not guarantee inclusion or citations.

How can we compare two audits fairly?

Use the same buyer questions, engines, visible model versions, languages and known context, with recorded dates and evidence coverage. Report unavailable settings and source panels explicitly; answer variation still limits causal conclusions.
