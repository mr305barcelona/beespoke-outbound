Google Ads budget planning

What does Google Ads management actually cost?

Google Ads management cost is the fee paid to the person or agency operating the account. It is separate from the money paid to Google for advertising. A useful B2B budget also includes tracking, landing pages, creative, software and internal sales time. Compare quotes on the same responsibilities and judge the total cost per qualified opportunity, rather than choosing the lowest management fee.

Compare four management fee models

Hourly billing makes the time allocation visible; confirm the minimum, approvals and what happens when work exceeds the allocation. A fixed fee provides budget predictability but needs limits for campaigns, markets and website work. A percentage of spend grows with the media budget, so ask what additional work that increase buys. A hybrid can combine these models; list every minimum and variable charge before comparing it with a simpler quote.

Reusable budget worksheet

Calculate three costs before comparing quotes

Use the same billing period and outcome definition for every provider.

1Total acquisition investment

Media + management + setup allocation + implementation + software + sales time.

2Cost per accepted opportunity

Total investment ÷ opportunities accepted by sales. Deduplicate inquiries and exclude spam, suppliers and job applicants.

3Cost per customer

Total investment ÷ customers from the same acquisition cohort. Allow for the sales cycle before judging profitability.

A zero denominator makes the result undefined. Do not compare platform form counts with another provider’s qualified opportunities.

Build a complete budget

Write separate lines for media, management, setup, conversion tracking, landing-page implementation, creative, reporting tools and client time. Mark each line as included, extra or owned internally. An audit is not implementation, and a landing-page recommendation is not a published page. Keep the advertising account and billing under your company’s ownership. Google’s average daily budget is not a strict daily cap; inspect the platform’s spending-limit documentation when converting it into a monthly plan.

Worked example: cheap leads can be expensive

Illustration only: over four weeks, $4,000 of advertising and $3,960 of management produce 40 inquiries, of which sales accepts eight as opportunities. Before other costs, the total is $7,960: $199 per inquiry and $995 per accepted opportunity. If two become customers, acquisition cost is $3,980 per customer. Add implementation, software and sales time before comparing this with customer gross profit. If no opportunities are accepted, cost per opportunity is undefined; it is not zero.

Choose the first test by what it must prove

Start with one offer, one buyer group and one measurable conversion path. Estimate whether the planned spend can purchase enough relevant clicks to inspect intent and lead quality. Test the form, Calendly flow and qualification feedback before bidding. Stop or revise when queries are irrelevant, tracking is broken or sales rejects the inquiries. Expand only after the evidence supports the next decision; a small sample is not a reliable forecast of revenue.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Google Ads management fee the advertising budget?

No. Management pays for work on the account; media spend pays Google for advertising. A written quote should show both separately, with setup, tracking, creative and software inclusions clearly identified.
