A B2B sales prospecting plan you can actually run

A B2B sales prospecting plan connects the offer, target accounts and people, evidence, channel, available time, approvals and sales handoff. It should tell an owner what happens next, what must be checked first and when to stop. Start with the completed example below, then adapt the schedule and optional private planner to your own market.

Choose the decision before the activity target

A useful plan answers a practical question: can this offer start a relevant conversation with this buyer, and can we handle that conversation properly? A sector list and a weekly send target do not answer it. Write down the offer, market, receiving sales owner and the evidence that would justify continuing.

Fictional objective: over a first four-week cycle, test whether operations owners at multi-site B2B testing firms find a coordination discussion relevant. The commercial owner reviews accepted account samples, actual replies and handoff quality at the end. A booked call is recorded separately from attendance and qualification. The decision is to continue, revise the segment or hold—not to declare success after a target number of sends.

Keep an aspiration, a commitment and a forecast separate. “We want five qualified calls” is an aspiration. “The owner has approved these research and reply blocks” is a commitment. A forecast needs comparable, mature historical cohorts and honest uncertainty. With no such history, record the forecast as unknown.

Make target-list approval a real decision

Record the company’s operating model, market and relevant role separately. Keep the source and date beside the fact. A contact-data match can help locate someone; it does not verify buyer fit or a need. In Beespoke’s process, the client’s judgment about whether we are targeting the right people is essential.

Assign one account owner and connect every contact to that account. Do not automatically enrol several colleagues in parallel sequences. A referral, correction or decline must reach the people operating the other channels. There is no required stakeholder count. A smaller business may have one person covering several roles.

Show the person why this particular approach makes sense

Personalization can come from a public post, relevant location or event, shared professional context, role or company news. It does not have to be an announcement about the company. The connection must be true and useful; a location match does not prove a business problem, and a public detail is not an invitation to be intrusive.

Fictional email — business context

Subject: Coordination between testing sites Hi [First name], The note about your next testing site raises a practical question: will the teams coordinate work in the same way as they do now? We help specialist service firms put the ownership and handover steps into a short working plan. I do not know whether this is an issue for your team; I wondered whether an outline of the decisions to check would be useful while the new site takes shape. Would you like me to send that outline? [Sender]

Why this fits the plan: the opening uses the actual cue, the offer explains a practical contribution, and the question leaves need unconfirmed. The sender must genuinely be able to provide the outline. Replace the structure with individual reasoning, not a mail merge that changes only the company name.

Fictional LinkedIn message — a public post

Hi [First name], the point in your post about preserving handover quality between locations is a useful one. We work on the ownership and follow-through around that kind of process. Would a short outline of the questions to settle before changing it be useful?

The second message has a different reason and a shorter format. Neither invents a result, shared relationship or urgency. “I saw” is not automatically wrong and an unusual synonym is not automatically human. Remove vague praise and unnecessary setup; check whether the specific reference and the offer belong together. The copywriting guide develops that editing process.

Record the audience’s language, working time zone, sender identity, reply owner and permitted alternative. Choose timing from the audience and actual experience; no universal weekday or hour can resolve a weak reason to write.

Plan a short sequence, then respond to what happens

Noah’s usual starting approach is an individualized opening and one brief follow-up, often around four days later. That is a working example, not a mandatory interval or a claim that two messages always suffice. Further contact depends on context, relevance and the agreed boundaries.

Budget the work before choosing the volume

Keep the simple research-envelope example: 600 available weekly minutes minus 180 for reply/review and 120 for coordination leaves 300 for research. At an assumed 20 minutes per account, that means 15 researched accounts; at 30 minutes, it means 10. This deliberately simplified envelope is not an all-in staffing plan: copy, setup and execution must also have time somewhere.

At an assumed 20 research minutes per account, the remaining 240 supports 12 accounts. At 30 minutes, capacity falls to 8. If each account needs another contact and that adds 5 research minutes, use 25 minutes per account: floor(240 ÷ 25) = 9, with 15 minutes left. Copy and reply reserves must still be sufficient for the extra people.

The reviewer may be the constraint even when a researcher has free time. Three available discovery slots are receiving capacity, not a prediction of three bookings. If actual reply or handoff work exceeds the reserve, reduce new activity and agree coverage. Record required software/data spend separately from internal time; do not assume a licence or extra channel is included.

Carry the actual reply through to a useful next step

Fictional reply

I oversee the workflow, but we are not replacing our current supplier. A discussion about the next site might help next quarter.

Confirmed: the role, the current supplier constraint and suggested timing. Unknown: a project, need, budget and purchasing authority. A useful clarification could be: “Would it be better to send the outline now, or reconnect closer to planning for the next site?” Do not claim they want to switch suppliers.

Human-contact reply rate is 14 ÷ 48 = 29.17% of initially delivered contacts. Responding-account rate is 12 ÷ 40 = 30% of approached accounts. Original bookings per initially delivered contact are 3 ÷ 48 = 6.25%. Each answers a different question. One qualified call out of one held call is a very small observation, not evidence of a stable conversion rate.

Compare complete periods under the same definitions and record material changes in offer, channel, staffing or tracking. A website visit assisted by an outbound message remains outreach-origin where that origin is known. A calendar click or confirmation-page visit is not a booking. Match real booking identity and source evidence where available; keep unknown origin unknown.

Separate internal test visits from prospect traffic; browser checks do not reconstruct real Analytics sessions. A fictional cohort cannot forecast your campaign or website bookings.

A high cost per meeting does not prove a dialer problem. A tiny or immature sample does not establish a winning channel. Fix a confirmed workflow defect promptly; describe a performance explanation as a hypothesis until evidence supports it.

Simple experiment note: record the segment and dates, hypothesis, one changed element, unchanged conditions, owner, outcome definition and review/stop rule. For example, test whether a source-specific coordination question produces more relevant conversations than a generic capabilities opening. Record exclusions, workload and uncertainty, not just the better-looking percentage. If several things changed, say so and avoid a causal claim.

Frequently asked questions
What belongs in a B2B sales prospecting plan?
The offer, objective and review period; target account and role criteria; exclusions and evidence; channels and messages; realistic workload; owners and approvals; reply, booking and sales handoff; outcome definitions and stopping conditions.
Is there a free prospecting-plan template?
Yes. The completed one-page example and its headings form an ungated outline. The optional private planner creates editable notes and copyable or printable text without an account. Reloading clears its inputs.
How many accounts should I prospect each week?
Start with observed research, message, approval and reply workload. The fictional 600-minute examples illustrate a calculation, not a recommended volume. Extra contacts and limited sales capacity change what is feasible.
Should LinkedIn or email come first?
Choose from the person’s relevant public activity, suitable reachability, authorized sender access and agreed scope. Neither visible activity nor a verified address guarantees a response or establishes buying intent.
How many follow-ups should the plan include?
Noah commonly starts with an individualized opening and one brief follow-up, often around four days later. Context, responses, suppression and authorization determine any further step; there is no universal touch minimum.
Does completing the planner authorize a launch?
No. The planner records user-entered information and missing dependencies. Provided information is not verified approval; campaign-launch authorization is separate from planning-document approval.
How is this different from account-based prospecting?
Account-based prospecting develops account selection, evidence and buying-role understanding. This operating plan connects that work to workload, approvals, messages, reply handling and sales handoff.
Can this plan guarantee meetings or forecast my results?
No. Capacity is not conversion. A forecast needs comparable mature history and uncertainty; fictional examples and a completed plan do not predict replies, qualified calls or revenue.
