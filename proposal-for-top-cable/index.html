Execution model

The first 90 days should validate lanes before scaling volume.

1

Confirm territories

Exclude current distributors, active customers, restricted markets, and product categories that are not priority.

2

Build account routes

Map senior and mid-level routes across procurement, engineering, project delivery, channel, and supplier quality.

3

Run specific outreach

Use email and LinkedIn messages tied to project delivery, certification, logistics, and energy infrastructure demand.

4

Route replies

Qualify application, territory, project timing, distributor fit, and whether the next step needs sales or technical support.
