# Beespoke SEO authority plan

## Objective

Earn relevant editorial mentions that strengthen the cost/pricing and cybersecurity clusters. Links must be earned through useful evidence, expert contribution, or a genuine commercial relationship—not purchased placements or bulk directory submissions.

## Assets to promote

1. **Outbound agency cost and break-even calculator**
   - Destination: `/guides/outbound-lead-generation-cost/`
   - Pitch: a transparent, editable model for comparing agency, outsourced SDR, and internal costs.
   - Best audiences: B2B sales newsletters, founder communities, RevOps publications, agency-comparison guides, startup finance content.

2. **Cybersecurity outbound evidence and segmentation framework**
   - Destinations: `/industries/cybersecurity-lead-generation/` and `/case-studies/cybersecurity-linkedin-lead-generation/`
   - Pitch: why “CISO” is not an ICP, with a practical buying-context framework and bounded first-party campaign evidence.
   - Best audiences: cybersecurity founder communities, security sales newsletters, GTM podcasts, accelerator resource libraries, vendor-marketing publications.

3. **Outsourced SDR operating-model comparison**
   - Destination: `/guides/outsourced-sdr-vs-lead-generation-agency/`
   - Pitch: a vendor-neutral framework comparing in-house SDR, outsourced SDR, lead-generation agency, and fractional sales leadership.
   - Best audiences: SaaS founder resources, sales-operations communities, HR/recruiting comparisons, fractional leadership networks.

## 90-day execution

### Weeks 1–2: proof packaging

- Request permission for one anonymized cybersecurity campaign screenshot and a bounded date range.
- Document the denominator behind the weekly meeting result where confidentiality permits.
- Turn the cost calculator methodology into a one-page downloadable reference.
- Prepare three concise expert biographies: outbound pricing, LinkedIn prospecting, and cybersecurity GTM.

### Weeks 3–6: relationship-led outreach

- Build a list of 40 highly relevant publications, newsletters, podcasts, communities, accelerators, and partner companies.
- Prioritize pages already discussing outbound cost, SDR outsourcing, cybersecurity sales, or LinkedIn prospecting.
- Offer a specific missing statistic, framework, calculator, or expert comment for each target.
- Ask existing clients, partners, podcast hosts, accelerators, and portfolio communities to reference the most relevant resource when it genuinely helps their audience.

### Weeks 7–12: compound winners

- Publish one data-led update using anonymized campaign patterns rather than generic advice.
- Contribute expert commentary to relevant journalist and newsletter requests.
- Pursue podcast appearances that produce a show-notes link to the appropriate guide.
- Refresh the best-performing page when Search Console reveals new query language.

## Outreach standard

Every message must answer:

- Why this exact publication or community?
- What is missing or outdated in its existing resource?
- What original evidence or tool can Beespoke contribute?
- Which single page is the most useful citation?

Do not use generic guest-post blasts, reciprocal-link schemes, paid-follow links, fabricated statistics, or irrelevant directories.

## Measurement

Track monthly:

- New referring domains and destination pages
- Editorial relevance and link placement quality
- Impressions and average position for cost, pricing, SDR, and cybersecurity queries
- Non-brand clicks and assisted Calendly conversions
- Pages moving into positions 4–20, which are the first refresh and link-building priorities
