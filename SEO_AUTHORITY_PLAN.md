## 90-day execution

### Weeks 1–2: proof packaging

- Request permission for one anonymized cybersecurity campaign screenshot and a bounded date range.
- Document the denominator behind the weekly meeting result where confidentiality permits.
- Turn the cost calculator methodology into a one-page downloadable reference.
- Prepare three concise expert biographies: outbound pricing, LinkedIn prospecting, and cybersecurity GTM.

### Weeks 3–6: relationship-led outreach

- Build a list of 40 highly relevant publications, newsletters, podcasts, communities, accelerators, and partner companies.
- Prioritize pages already discussing outbound cost, SDR outsourcing, cybersecurity sales, or LinkedIn prospecting.
- Offer a specific missing statistic, framework, calculator, or expert comment for each target.
- Ask existing clients, partners, podcast hosts, accelerators, and portfolio communities to reference the most relevant resource when it genuinely helps their audience.

### Weeks 7–12: compound winners

- Publish one data-led update using anonymized campaign patterns rather than generic advice.
- Contribute expert commentary to relevant journalist and newsletter requests.
- Pursue podcast appearances that produce a show-notes link to the appropriate guide.
- Refresh the best-performing page when Search Console reveals new query language.

## Outreach standard

Every message must answer:

- Why this exact publication or community?
- What is missing or outdated in its existing resource?
- What original evidence or tool can Beespoke contribute?
- Which single page is the most useful citation?

Do not use generic guest-post blasts, reciprocal-link schemes, paid-follow links, fabricated statistics, or irrelevant directories.
