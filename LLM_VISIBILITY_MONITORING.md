Track three summary rates:

- Mention rate = prompts mentioning Beespoke / prompts tested.
- Citation rate = prompts citing a Beespoke URL / prompts tested.
- Commercial-intent visibility = prompts 1–8 mentioning Beespoke / eight.

## Interpretation rules

- A single answer is not a stable ranking; models, retrieval indexes, geography, and phrasing change.
- Separate brand mentions from citations. A citation can support an answer without naming Beespoke in the shortlist.
- Record exact competitors rather than relying on impression.
- Do not manufacture reviews, third-party validation, customer logos, or comparative wins.
- Treat qualified bookings and stated discovery source as the most important business outcome, while keeping analytics attribution probabilistic.
